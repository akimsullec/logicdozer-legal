About this policy

LogicDozer is developed by BORIS AKIMENKO, an individual entrepreneur registered in Georgia. The public business contact details appear below. This policy covers the Android app and this documentation website. The app is intended for people aged 13 and over; it is not directed to children under 13. The current app does not verify a user's age.

Games and local storage

Games, translations and sounds work offline. Language, appearance, play mode, sound preferences, progress and results are stored on your device. LogicDozer has no user registration, own game-progress server, purchases or subscriptions in the current version. We do not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local game data; updates are designed to preserve it. You can export and restore progress using the system file picker. The backup contains your game progress and preferences. You choose where to save it; a selected cloud storage provider may process the file under its own terms. LogicDozer does not automatically upload these backups to its own server. Uninstalling the app does not delete previously exported files; manage or delete them in the chosen storage location.

Advertising and Google services

The app uses Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP). Online ad requests can transmit IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app/ad interactions, technical and performance information and identifiers permitted by the SDK configuration to Google. Android advertising-ID permission is removed. Ads are requested with restrictive age treatment and without personalization. This does not mean that no data is processed. Google uses advertising-service data for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention, subject to its policies and the request configuration. Offline play does not require ad loading.

Choices and lawful processing

Age 13+ is an audience declaration, not proof of adulthood or consent. Personalized advertising is postponed. Where legally required, valid consent or another applicable legal ground must exist before the relevant processing; reading this policy or using the app is not blanket consent. When UMP requires a privacy-options entry, it appears in the app settings. You can play offline and use Android privacy controls. Necessary local settings support the requested game functions. We use support messages to answer requests and resolve problems, relying on our legitimate interest in providing support where that ground applies, or another ground required by applicable law.

Support and retention

If you email logicdozer@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and attachments and use them to handle your request. Do not send passwords, access codes, banking details or unnecessary personal data. Support correspondence is kept for 6 months after the case is closed, then deleted. Specific information may be retained longer only as necessary for a legal obligation or legal claims. Gmail processes email under Google's applicable terms. Local game data remains on the device until you remove it. Google's separate retention rules apply to information it processes; deleting the app does not automatically delete that information.

Your data rights

Contact logicdozer@gmail.com to ask about personal data or exercise applicable rights, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent where those rights apply. We may request only information needed to verify the request and your authority. We respond within the applicable legal deadline. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing. You may complain to the competent data-protection authority. For data Google independently controls, a request to Google may also be needed; its privacy tools are linked below. There is no LogicDozer account to delete.

Recipients and international processing

Google provides advertising and email services; GitHub hosts this website. Their processing may occur in the United States and other countries, outside your country and Georgia. Applicable contractual terms, transfer mechanisms and safeguards depend on the service and law; the providers' policies explain them. Contact us for information about transfers for which we are responsible. We do not operate our own game-data backend. We do not sell support correspondence or use it for advertising profiles.

Website and changes

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts or forms to these pages. GitHub may process IP address and technical request logs to provide and secure hosting under its privacy statement. External links open services with their own privacy practices. We publish changes to this policy at this address and revise the version/date. New features or materially different processing require a review of this notice and any necessary consent. Contact us using the public details below.

Yandex advertising

The Android app also integrates Yandex Mobile Ads through AdMob mediation. Online ad requests expose the IP address and technical request information to the receiving service, which returns advertising materials. We disable personalized-ad consent, location tracking and App Ad Analytics reporting in the Yandex SDK; the Android advertising-ID permission is removed. These settings do not mean that no data is processed. The service provider's privacy policy describes its processing, retention, international transfers and data-rights contacts. Removing LogicDozer does not automatically delete information held by advertising providers. Contact logicdozer@gmail.com for questions about our integration. Games remain available offline without loading ads.
